# Housing Navigator

Vocabulary for assessing a proposed housing project using property evidence, explicit assumptions and sourced findings.

## Language

**Parcel**:
A land record identified by its source parcel identifier and jurisdiction. A parcel boundary is not a survey.

**Proposal**:
A description of intended work and use on a site, including confirmed user inputs and unresolved details.
_Avoid_: Approved plan

**Evidence**:
Information attributed to a source, with available source dates and retrieval dates. Conflicting evidence remains distinct.

**Assumption**:
A user-supplied or explicitly hypothetical statement used to explore a proposal, without treating it as verified evidence.

**Finding**:
A sourced explanation of an applicable check, its review status, and the next action or missing information.
_Avoid_: Approval, feasibility verdict

**Comparison**:
An account of the differences between proposals, distinguishing changes in explanation, review status and next action.

**Coverage**:
The jurisdictions, proposal types and checks that the product can evaluate. Accepting an input does not establish evaluation coverage.

**Brief**:
An exportable record of a proposal, its evidence, assumptions, findings and unresolved questions.

**Packet**:
A collection of project documents supplied by a user, potentially containing plans, reports and permit records.

**Next action**:
A concrete task that advances the project by addressing a finding or obtaining missing evidence, with an identified responsible party.
_Avoid_: Generic referral

**Project path**:
The saved sequence and dependencies of next actions for a project, allowing the user to resume after completing work.
