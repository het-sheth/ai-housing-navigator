# Preliminary Development Ease Score: Pittsburgh proposal

Status: provisional product rubric and implemented evidence API, September 26, 2026. The organizer's Track 1 brief asks for a source-grounded Development Ease Score with explained zoning, environmental and infrastructure barriers. The planned score concerns documented friction for an explicit proposal. It is neither an approval probability nor financial feasibility.

`POST /api/screening/run` accepts an exact Allegheny County parcel ID and explicit `activities`, `proposedHomes`, `housingForm`, and `groundDisturbance`. It returns `pending` until the exact parcel and full-parcel Pittsburgh municipality are confirmed, the supported use-table case is checked, and **every required rubric component** is assessed. Other county parcels remain valid intake but are pending for this Pittsburgh rubric. No assessment classification or fixed Lanark example supplies a proposed use. Pending responses repeat the proposal, rubric version, named check statuses, source URLs, retrieval time, unknown source dates, and next actions. They expose no score, range, per-check points, or numeric weights. Runs are not yet stored as immutable history.

The numeric rubric below is an internal design proposal, not current user-facing output. A score is withheld whenever any required check is missing, failed, unsupported, stale, or unresolved. The present integrations always leave zoning overlays and dimensions, undermining, process, and infrastructure unassessed, so they always return `pending` with evidence and actions. Weights are provisional product choices, not official organizer weights or calibrated outcomes.

| Component | Current provisional treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Zoning, 40 | A source-checked Pittsburgh R1D district record marked `Approved`, the published City use table, one explicitly proposed detached home, and no mixed or additional dwelling activity earn **15** for a bounded use-table screen. The other **25** remain `[0,25]` for overlays, dimensions, lawful baseline and City interpretation. The layer's `Approved` field describes the map record, not project approval. This screen grants no permission verdict. |
| Mapped hazards, 30 | FEMA flood zone **12**: a single minimal-hazard Zone X feature covering the whole parcel earns 12; an intersecting SFHA or A/V zone earns 0; absent or partial coverage stays `[0,12]`. City 25 percent slope **10**: no mapped intersecting feature earns 10; a mapped intersection earns 0 with proposed disturbance, 5 without it, or `[0,10]` if disturbance is unknown. These are product screening anchors, not survey findings or proof that work overlaps slope. Undermining **8** remains `[0,8]` because no adapter runs yet. |
| Proposal review path, 15 | `[0,15]` until the actual combined work scope and current City process are reviewed. Permit history alone is insufficient. |
| Infrastructure and access, 15 | `[0,15]` until parcel-specific utility capacity and access are confirmed. Nearby roads or a user answer cannot establish capacity. |

Source errors remain independent: a FEMA failure does not erase a completed City slope or zoning finding. A source with unknown vintage is identified as such. A favorable map result means only that this layer supplied the bounded observation stated in its check, not that the site is safe. Source conflicts and whole-parcel ambiguity leave the relevant checks unresolved. A future numeric result requires all components assessed against accepted source and rule versions. Adverse mapped flags and high-priority follow-ups remain visible while the score is withheld.

The app already retrieved County parcel and assessment observations. This slice adds live County municipality, Pittsburgh zoning, City slope, and FEMA flood queries plus a narrow published use-table mapping. It does not connect overlays, undermining, provider capacity, a permit route, or a parcel pro forma. A supported Mountford proposal can receive source findings and next actions, but no numeric score under the complete-rubric gate. No rule was professionally reviewed, so the use-table mapping is explicitly provisional.
